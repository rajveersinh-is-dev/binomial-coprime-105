# Problem selection

## Selected problem

Erdős problem #376: Are there infinitely many positive integers n such that
the central binomial coefficient C(2n,n) is coprime to 105 = 3·5·7?

Equivalently, by Kummer's theorem, are there infinitely many n whose base-3,
base-5, and base-7 expansions use only digits 0,1 (base 3), 0,1,2 (base 5),
and 0,1,2,3 (base 7)?

## Why this problem

- Genuine open problem (listed on erdosproblems.com; included in the
  FrontierMath Erdős benchmark of 68 curated open problems).
- Elementary statement; the decisive content is number-theoretic structure,
  not large-scale computation.
- Connects to the Bloom–Croot (2025) theory of integers with small digits
  in multiple bases.

## Open status

The problem is marked open. Bloom and Croot (arXiv:2509.02835) proved a
weak version for sufficiently large bases and established the relevant
heuristic condition; the case of the small bases 3,5,7 remains open.
